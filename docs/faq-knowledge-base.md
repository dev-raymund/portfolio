## 1. How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope, but here are the floors I work from:

- **Basic 3–5 page WordPress site:** starts at $1,500.
- **E-commerce store (Shopify):** starts at $3,000.
- **Custom design or larger sites (8+ pages):** starts at $3,000, usually lands in the $3,000–$8,000 range depending on complexity.
- **Complete website package** (site + branding + copy + SEO setup): starts at $2,500.

I quote a fixed price after we've talked through what you need, so you know the number before we start.

*Twin note: never go below these floors. If a prospect's budget is below floor, recommend a DIY tool (Squarespace, Wix) honestly — better for them than a bad fit with me.*

## 2. How long does a website take?

Typical timelines from kickoff to launch:

- Basic 3–5 page site: 3–4 weeks
- E-commerce store: 4–8 weeks
- Custom or larger builds: 6–10 weeks

The biggest factor is usually how quickly you can get me the content (text, images, product info). Sites get delayed by missing content far more often than by development.

## 3. Do you work with international clients? / Where are you based?

Yes — most of my clients are international. I'm based in the Philippines and work with clients worldwide, mostly in North America, Europe, Australia, and Southeast Asia. Time zones haven't been an issue; I keep flexible hours and respond within one business day.

## 4. What's included in the "complete website package"?

Everything a small business needs to launch a working site:

- **Website design and development** (WordPress or Shopify)
- **Branding** — logo, color system, and a visual identity that holds together
- **Copywriting** — homepage and core-page copy
- **On-page SEO setup** — meta tags, site structure, basic technical SEO

It's available for a flat price starting at $2,500, which is less than buying each piece separately. Most small businesses need all of these anyway, so it usually saves money and time.

Maintenance is separate — $100/month retainer if you want me to keep the site updated after launch.

## 5. Do you do maintenance / ongoing support?

Yes, through a monthly retainer. It covers:

- WordPress / Shopify and plugin updates
- Security monitoring and backups
- Small content changes (text edits, swapping images, updating product info)
- Quick responses to anything that breaks

Starts at $100/month. Larger sites or higher-volume change requests are quoted higher. No long-term contracts — month-to-month, cancel anytime.

## 6. Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes — redesigns are about half my work. The process is:

1. We talk about what's not working with the current site
2. I audit it and quote the redesign as a fixed price
3. We rebuild it on WordPress or Shopify (or keep the current platform if it makes sense)

Pricing is in the same range as new builds. Sometimes redesigns are cheaper because content already exists; sometimes more expensive because we're untangling existing problems. I'll know after I've seen the current site.

## 7. Do you build on [specific platform — Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, custom code]?

My primary stack is **WordPress and Shopify** — that's where I'm fastest and most clients land. I also work with:

- **Webflow** — for design-forward marketing sites
- **Custom code (HTML/CSS/JS, React)** — for bespoke builds
- **Wix and Squarespace** — for clients who want to maintain the site themselves after launch

If you've got a strong preference for a specific platform, tell me and I'll let you know honestly whether it's a good fit. Sometimes the answer is "yes, that works"; sometimes it's "you'd get more value from a different platform — here's why."

## 8. Do you offer hourly rates?

No — fixed scope or monthly retainer only. Two reasons:

- Hourly billing creates the wrong incentives. I'd rather be paid for the outcome than the hours.
- It's better for you. You know the total cost upfront and don't have to track my hours.

If the scope is unclear, we'll start with a paid discovery call ($150, credited toward the project if you book me) where I scope and quote the work properly.

---

## 9. What's the process? How do we get started?

Four steps:

1. **We talk.** A free 30-minute call to understand what you need and whether I'm the right fit. No pressure, no pitch.
2. **I scope and quote.** A written proposal with scope, timeline, and a fixed price. Usually within 2 business days of our call.
3. **We build.** I work in 1–2 week sprints and share progress as we go. You see the work as it's built.
4. **You launch.** Optional maintenance retainer so the site keeps working after launch.

To get started: send me a message describing what you need (rough idea is fine), and I'll come back within 2 business days with thoughts and next steps.

## 10. How do I pay? What about contracts?

- **Payment:** 50% upfront to start, 50% on launch. For larger projects (over $5,000) we can split into three payments.
- **Methods:** Wise, PayPal, or bank transfer. USD for international clients, PHP for local.
- **Contract:** Yes — every project has a written agreement covering scope, timeline, payment terms, and what happens if either of us needs to end early. Simple and standard, no legalese.
- **Revisions:** Two rounds of revisions included per page/section. Beyond that is charged at a change-order rate.
