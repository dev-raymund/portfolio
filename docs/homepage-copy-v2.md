## Process section

**Heading:**
> ## How it works

**Body — four short steps:**

> **1. We talk.** A 30-minute call to understand what you need and figure out whether I'm the right fit for the project. No pitch, no pressure — sometimes the honest answer is "you don't need a custom build, here's a tool that would work for you."
>
> **2. I scope and quote.** A written proposal with scope, timeline, and a fixed price. Usually within 2 business days of our call. You know the total before we start.
>
> **3. We build.** I work in 1–2 week sprints and share progress as we go. You see the site take shape, and we adjust based on what's actually working — not what we guessed at the start.
>
> **4. You launch — and I'm still around.** Optional maintenance retainer so the site keeps working after launch. No long-term contracts.

*Intent: each step now has a small bit of *teaching* attached. Step 1's "sometimes the honest answer is..." reinforces the educator stance and pre-empts the "is this guy going to upsell me?" worry. Step 2's "you know the total before we start" is reassurance language. Step 3 frames sprints as adaptive, not rigid. Step 4 emphasizes "no long-term contracts" — small but important friction-remover.*

## Notes for implementation

**Layout direction:**
- Hero takes the full first screen on desktop
- Services section is the heaviest block — give it room
- Case studies are the second-heaviest — visual, not text-heavy
- About and Process can be lighter
- One CTA repeats: top-right of nav, end of hero, end of homepage

**Tone consistency check (run this when editing):**
- "I" for the business, "we" for the work together is fine (matches voice doc)
- No "passionate," "innovative," "cutting-edge," "synergy," "ecosystem"
- No exclamation points
- Acknowledge → explain → state pattern in any section that pushes back on an alternative
- Every section answers a question the prospect is silently asking — never just exists for completeness

**SEO basics for the homepage:**
- Title tag: *Raymund Hermoso — WordPress & Shopify Web Designer for Small Businesses*
- Meta description: *Independent web designer and developer with 5+ years and 100+ sites shipped. WordPress, Shopify, and custom builds for small businesses worldwide.*
- H1: the headline above
- H2s: section headings as written

**What to keep editing forever:**
- The hero subhead — A/B test variants over time
- The services pricing — update floors every 6–12 months
- The case studies — replace weakest one whenever you ship a stronger project
