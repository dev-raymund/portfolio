- Agencies looking to white-label (different business model)
- Race-to-the-bottom Fiverr-style buyers

## The complete-with-the-website bundle

Positioned as: *"Everything your website needs to actually work, not just look good."*

| Service | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Branding** | Logo, color system, basic visual identity for the site |
| **Copywriting** | Homepage and core page copy that sells, not just describes |
| **SEO setup** | On-page SEO, meta tags, site structure, basic technical SEO |
| **Maintenance** | Updates, backups, security, small content changes |

Each can be sold standalone, but the bundle is the lead offer. Most small businesses need all of these; bundling them is cheaper for them and more profitable for you than selling them piecemeal.

## Pricing floors

International USD. Floors, not averages — quote higher when scope justifies it.

| Project type | Floor | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| Basic 3–5 page WordPress site | $1,500 | $1,500 – $3,000 |
| E-commerce (Shopify) | $3,000 | $3,000 – $7,000 |
| Custom design / 8+ pages / complex functionality | $3,000 | $3,000 – $8,000+ |
| Branding add-on | $500 | $500 – $1,500 |
| Copywriting add-on | $400 | $400 – $1,500 (per page or per project) |
| SEO setup add-on | $300 | $300 – $1,000 |
| Maintenance retainer | $100/mo | $100 – $400/mo |
| **Complete bundle (site + branding + copy + SEO)** | $2,500 | $2,500 – $5,000+ |

**Floor rules:**
- Never go below the floor for any reason. If a client can't afford the floor, they're not your client — refer them to a DIY tool.
- Always quote ranges, not single numbers, until scope is locked.
- For local PH clients, peso pricing is set separately (typically 40–60% of USD floors). Document those in a separate sheet when ready.
- Hourly is not offered. Fixed scope or monthly retainer only.

## Voice & positioning

**Voice:** Quietly confident. Direct, no jargon, lets the experience do the work.

**Positioning statement (the one-liner the whole site builds around):**

> *"I build WordPress and Shopify websites for small businesses that want a professional online presence. 5+ years, 100+ sites shipped."*

**Why this works:**
- "WordPress and Shopify" → searchable, recognizable, signals specialty without being narrow
- "Small businesses" → clear audience
- "Professional online presence" → the outcome they're hiring for
- "5+ years, 100+ sites" → instantly separates you from the 1-year freelancer flood
- No adjectives like "passionate," "creative," "innovative" — those are filler that everyone uses

## What I want to be known for (3-year horizon)

- The dependable WordPress/Shopify person for small businesses internationally
- Someone who ships, doesn't disappear, and treats client work like a craft

## Differentiators (the things to lean on)

1. **5+ years, 100+ sites.** Most international freelancers have less than 2 years. Lead with this.
2. **Mix of freelance + agency/employment experience.** Means you've seen how teams work, not just solo projects.
3. **Full bundle in one place.** Most freelancers offer one piece (just dev, just design); having branding + copy + SEO + maintenance under one roof is rare and valuable for small businesses without in-house teams.
4. **PH-based pricing advantage.** Can deliver quality at rates that undercut US/EU freelancers without competing on race-to-the-bottom platforms.

---

## Anti-positioning (what you are NOT)

- Not an agency. Don't use "we." Use "I."
- Not a junior. Don't compete on price with 1-year freelancers.
- Not a Fiverr template-flipper. The work is custom, even on WordPress.
- Not a code-only developer. The bundle exists because business owners need more than code.
- Not a marketer or growth consultant. You build the site and set up SEO; you don't run ad campaigns or do content strategy.

## What changes when this changes

Update this one-pager when:
- You stop wanting to take a category of work

- You add or drop a stack from the primary
- Your audience focus changes
